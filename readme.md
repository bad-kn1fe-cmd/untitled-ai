# Untitled AI

> An open-source, mobile-first AI roleplay engine built with Kotlin & Android. Free from corporate restrictions, hidden fees, and privacy leaks. 

**Untitled AI** is an independent, community-driven roleplay engine for Android. Designed to run smoothly on your device while putting full control over characters, scenarios, and model configurations directly into your hands. Bring your own API keys, customize your prompts, and craft stories without artificial limits.

---

## Features

* **Mobile-First Experience:** Native Android app built for smooth interaction, responsive UI, and offline-first storage.
* **Bring Your Own Keys (BYOK):** Connect your preferred AI models and providers directly. Zero platform fees, zero middleman subscriptions.
* **Total Freedom:** Uncensored story generation tailored to your system prompts and preferences.
* **Character & Context Management:** Create, import, and organize custom personas, world lore, and roleplay scenarios.
* **Open Source & Private:** No telemetry tracking your prompts. Your data stays on your device.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Platform:** Android
* **Language:** Kotlin
* **Build System:** Gradle (Kotlin DSL `.kts`)
* **Architecture:** Modern Android development practices (Jetpack Compose / MVVM)
